My perspective

Useful technology must survive contact with the physical world.

My work crosses software, electronics and field operations. I am interested in systems where sensing, computation and human decisions meet—from edge AI and embedded control to mobile interfaces and cloud data.

Commercial agricultural work adds direct experience with budgets, planning, people, equipment and limited resources.

02

Working principles

Start with constraints

Power, memory, bandwidth, cost and maintainability shape engineering decisions from the beginning.

Connect the layers

I think across hardware, firmware, data, application interfaces and the operator’s real workflow.

Build evidence

Prototypes are described honestly: what was built, which technologies were used and what remains to be verified.

Design for use

Technical sophistication matters only when a system is clear, dependable and useful to the people operating it.
